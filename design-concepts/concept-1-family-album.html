The Family Album

The whole app becomes a scrapbook someone's daughter could have glued together at the kitchen table. Paper grain, photo corners, washi tape, handwritten notes in the margins. Nothing floats on a gradient; everything is pinned, taped, or tucked in. Warmth through craft, not through softness filters.
