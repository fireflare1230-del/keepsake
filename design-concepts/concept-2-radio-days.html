Design pulled from the world the patients actually grew up in: 1950s radios, diner menus, thick friendly outlines, sunburst clocks, walnut and burnt orange. Bold, high-contrast, and joyful, like a Wes Anderson prop department built a care app. Every shape is an arch or a pill, nothing is a plain rectangle.
