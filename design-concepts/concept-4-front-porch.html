Front Porch at Dusk

A storybook nightfall: paper-cut silhouettes of a house with one warm lit window, fireflies drifting, lamplight gold on deep evening blue. The only dark concept, and dark for a reason: evenings are the hard hours in dementia care (sundowning), and this palette is built to soothe them. Feels like the cover of a beloved children's book, painted by hand.
